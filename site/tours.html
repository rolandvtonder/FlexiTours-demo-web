Cape Peninsula

Cape Peninsula Sight Seeing Tour

From R1,800 per person

Your visit to Cape Town and South Africa is not complete until you go on this tour. Let us make you experience the real beauty of Cape Town and enjoy a glass of wine even as we take you to some of the award winning wine farms.

  1. Pick up from your hotel or accommodation.
  2. Drive around key historic landmarks in the Cape Town CBD.
  3. Table Mountain cable car station — stop for viewing and pictures.
  4. Drive along the Cape Peninsula with stops and passes at the Twelve Apostles, Camps Bay, Hout Bay and the Chapman's Peak scenic route.
  5. Cape Point Vineyards stop for an optional quick wine tasting, drinks and refreshments.
  6. Cape of Good Hope and Cape Point with a light optional sit-down lunch or snack.
  7. Boulders Beach penguin colony for direct encounters with the penguins.
  8. Return to Cape Town CBD via Muizenberg beach to end the tour.

Winelands

Cape Winelands Wine Tasting Tour

From R1,500 per person

Explore the Cape Winelands, a picturesque region nestled in the heart of South Africa, renowned for its breathtaking landscapes, world-class vineyards and rich cultural heritage. Embark on a captivating journey through three of the most enchanting towns in this wine lover's paradise: Paarl, Franschhoek and Stellenbosch.

  1. Pick up from your hotel or accommodation.
  2. Wine tasting at one of the best vineyards in the Paarl Valley.
  3. Proceed to Spice Route in Paarl and try various experiences — craft beer, chocolates and spices.
  4. Explore a wine farm in Franschhoek for more tasting and an optional lunch.
  5. Proceed to Stellenbosch for more wine tasting.
  6. Return to Cape Town for drop-off at your hotel or accommodation.

City & coast

Best of Cape Town Tour

Price on request

The Best of Cape Town Tour offers a comprehensive exploration of this vibrant South African city, known for its stunning natural beauty, rich history and diverse culture.

  1. Pick up from your hotel or accommodation.
  2. Drive along Camps Bay and Llandudno to Hout Bay.
  3. Proceed to Cape Point Nature Reserve via the world-famous Chapman's Peak Drive.
  4. Continue to Boulders Beach to view the penguins.
  5. Head on to Simon's Town to tour the Historical Mile. Lunch is on your own account.
  6. End the day by driving to the Winelands of Stellenbosch and visiting a farm for some wine tasting.
  7. Return to the pick-up point.

Adventure

West Coast Adventure Tour

From R2,500 per person

Embark on a thrilling adventure that seamlessly combines the adrenaline-pumping experience of quad biking on the Atlantis Sand Dunes with the sophisticated pleasures of wine tasting at the renowned Durbanville wine farms.

  1. Pick up from your hotel or accommodation.
  2. Feel the rush as you navigate the Atlantis sand dunes on a quad bike for 1 hour.
  3. Optional extra: experience the rush of gliding down steep dunes on a sandboard for 1 hour.
  4. Optional extra: head to the Durbanville Wine Valley, a picturesque wine region known for its boutique wineries.
  5. Return to Cape Town for drop-off at your hotel or accommodation.

Big 5

Cape Safari Tour — Big 5 Option

Price on request

This safari tour is a two-hour drive from Cape Town and has all of the Big Five: rhino, elephant, lion, buffalo and leopard. Embark on exhilarating game drives through renowned private game reserves. Visitors can also see many other native African species, including giraffe, zebra, hippo, ostrich, eland, wildebeest and springbok.

  1. Pick up from your hotel or accommodation.
  2. Start: Cape Town CBD at 09h00 and travel for two hours.
  3. Enjoy a welcome drink and buffet lunch.
  4. Embark on a 2–3 hour shared game drive.
  5. Depart the game reserve at 16h00–16h30.
  6. Return to Cape Town for drop-off at your hotel or accommodation.

Big 4

Cape Safari Tour — Big 4 Option

Price on request

This safari tour is a 45-minute drive from Cape Town and has four of the Big Five: lion, buffalo, rhino and leopard. Other animals you can see include cheetah, giraffe, zebra, kudu, eland, oryx, blue wildebeest, mountain reedbuck and red hartebeest. The reserve offers guided game drives, bush walks, birdwatching and mountain biking trails, with experienced guides providing insight into the flora, fauna and ecosystems of the area.

  1. Pick up from your hotel or accommodation.
  2. Start: Cape Town CBD at 09h00 and travel for 60 minutes.
  3. Enjoy a welcome drink and lunch.
  4. Embark on a 2–3 hour shared game drive.
  5. Explore other activities available at the game reserve for one to two more hours.
  6. Depart the game reserve at 15h30–16h00.
  7. Return to Cape Town for drop-off at your hotel or accommodation.
