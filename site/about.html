Our story

Built on local knowledge, not a call centre.

We have several years of experience in the events, tours and adventures industry. That means we know which route works on a windy morning, which farm is worth the extra half hour, and how long the queue at the cableway really takes.

Our tours take in historic neighbourhoods like Bo-Kaap, monuments, the magnificent Table Mountain — one of the seven natural wonders of the world — scenic views like Chapman's Peak, beautiful beaches, penguin encounters and vast wine farms.
